## 5. Story Blueprint: "Hero Kai & The Sixteen Guardians of San Jie Class" (《凱與三結班的十六位守護靈獸》)
Act I — The Sixteen Scrolls of Dawn (晨曦中的十六卷軸): Hero Kai, String, and the Baby Praying Mantis Spell Guardian discover sixteen glowing paper scrolls drifting down from the sky into Pythonia's Whispering Meadow—each drawn by a young creator from San Jie Class!
Act II — Awakening the Mid-Evolution Guardians (覺醒的守護靈獸): As Kai reads each child's wish aloud, the sketches leap off the parchment into their Mid-Evolution forms! Sticky splashes into a crystal stream; Berrie shares a glowing bowl of fresh fruit salad; OKX adjusts his tiny spectacles while DOYB and Javin Jr light up the meadow with starlight runes; Star-Yarn Caticorn rolls a rainbow comet of yarn to welcome everyone.
Act III — The Gray Eraser Fog (灰霧危機): A gloomy "Doubt Fog" drifts over the Valley of Forgotten Drawings, dimming the flowers and freezing the river. Rather than fighting with anger, Kai reminds the pets of the Three-Heart Spirit (自愛 · 愛人 · 愛大地):
Love Earth (愛大地): 雷冰火鹿, Javin Jr, Sylvan-Fox, and Miku weave emerald roots, clear skies, and blooming moss to heal the soil.
Love Others (愛人): BARKLEY, 火冰狗, Poppy, Bro, Berrie, and Emerald-X Mecha warm the shivering valley, guide lost travelers with flame-feathered light, and share plant-based fruit harvests.
Love Self (自愛): Sticky, DOYB, 蛋 (Volt-Drake), OKX, 虛假元卡丘, and Star-Yarn Caticorn prove that being uniquely yourself—whether a water stickman, a stitched plushie, or a starry kitten—is the brightest magic of all!
Act IV — The Three-Heart Evolution Ceremony (三心共鳴進化): Together with Kai's glowing Tzu Chi Dharma-ship belt emblem, all 16 pets resonate into their Stage 3 Grand Guardian forms, turning the Gray Fog into a rainbow aurora!
Act V — The Gift Back to San Jie Class (送給三結班的禮物): Kai seals each pet's Evolution Card and sends a personal bilingual thank-you letter back across the stars to every single student of San Jie Class—leaving three warm, glowing Mystery Eggs ready for Uvincc, Ko Zi Xing, and Jacob whenever their scrolls arrive!
