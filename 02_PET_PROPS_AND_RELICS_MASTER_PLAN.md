## Prop Continuity Rules for Comic Panels
Stage Alignment: When a pet appears in Baby form, carry only its Stage 1 prop; in Mid/Site form, carry its Stage 2 prop; in Grand Resonance finale, unleash its Stage 3 prop.
Zero Modern Clutter: All props are rendered in warm Ghibli storybook watercolor/sepia ink (brass, woven cloth, jade, crystal, paper, wood, natural flora — never modern plastic or guns).
Bilingual Callout Compatibility: Props are drawn 100% text-free in raw panel art; any prop name callout is overlaid cleanly by the Python bilingual compositor.
